## Substitution policy

No British or Argentine stand-in may enter an allowlist or scenario ledger without recording:

1. the historical unit or role represented;
2. the in-game platform actually used;
3. materially different weapons, sensors, aviation, speed, endurance, survivability, or point value;
4. intended compensation through quantity, loadout, proficiency, readiness, ROE, positioning, objectives, or scenario timing.

A nation/flag override changes presentation only. It must never be described as changing the underlying platform capability.
